## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者完成：选题素材 → AI 写作 → 模板排版 → 多平台发布，一条流水线完成。

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，**内容 100% 过 AI 检测**。账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

### 资源管理

- **今日热点**：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作
- **微信爆文**：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
- **图片素材**：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图
- **我的图片**：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签
- **我的素材**：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用
- 素材完全自定义：用户自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改，素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定

### 创作工坊

**文章模式**

- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测
- 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器、自定义模板（另存为我的模板）
- 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台

**贴图模式**

- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类
- **自定义模板（高级版）**：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
- 贴图素材自定义：除了内置模板，用户可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题

### 账号中心

- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存
- 一键前往平台后台

### 内容发布

- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

### 自动托管

- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文

## English Overview

Wangaogao Hui is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It covers content scenarios such as WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijia, and Xiaohongshu (image posts), helping individual bloggers, agency teams, and content creators complete the pipeline: topic sourcing → AI writing → template styling → multi-platform publishing.

Articles generated are natural and free of obvious AI traces, **with a 100% pass rate on AI content detection**. Account credentials and creation data are stored locally on the user's machine and are not transmitted to third parties.

## English Features

### Resource Management

- **Today's Hot Topics**: Real-time updated trending articles for topic selection and secondary creation
- **WeChat Hits**: Aggregated viral articles from the WeChat ecosystem, one-click import into the Creation Studio
- **Image Library**: Platform-collected images with AI auto-tagging, supporting tag-based filtering and search; tagged images can be used as article illustrations
- **My Images**: Personal image library with image upload and custom tags
- **My Materials**: Import TXT materials or AI-generate outlines in batches based on account positioning; editable and usable by automated hosting
- Fully customizable materials: users build material groups, batch import, AI-generate, and edit each entry; materials are not limited to text and can be bound with specified layout/cover templates and directly linked to hosting tasks

### Creation Studio

**Article Mode**

- Input topic/material, AI automatically generates: viral-style title → outline → full body → layout
- Natural, human-like output with no obvious AI traces and a 100% pass rate on AI content detection
- Built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates with a theme generator and custom templates (save as My Templates)
- Fully customizable layout: modify colors, fonts, font sizes, and style parameters based on any built-in template, or write custom CSS; save as "My Templates" and use on par with built-in templates
- Auto image matching: select images from the image library matched to article content, or from the personal library
- Word count options: 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
- Rich-text editing: components such as headings, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, and CTAs
- Mobile preview and copy HTML to WeChat Official Account backend

**Image Post Mode**

- AI generates image-post title + segmented body + hashtags based on the topic
- Cover template + content card template; title rendered onto the cover, body segmented into cards
- 50+ built-in cover templates, categorized by style
- **Custom templates (Advanced)**: upload background images, configure text areas (position/font size/color/alignment/line count), and custom CSS; saved as My Templates for use across all platforms
- Custom image-post materials: in addition to built-in templates, users can upload their own cover/content card background images, freely define title and body text areas, and save them to the personal template library
- Publish image posts to WeChat Official Accounts with hashtags automatically included in the body

### Account Center

- QR code / web login supported for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijia (Xiaohongshu supports image-post publishing); cookies are saved automatically
- WeChat Official Accounts support syncing multiple accounts under the same name and marking the currently active account
- Account switches and configurations such as original-content/first-release/AI declaration are persisted
- One-click access to platform backends

### Content Publishing

- Unified publishing for articles/image posts: select platform first → then account (linked); supports publishing to WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijia; WeChat Official Accounts support saving drafts; image posts support publishing to Xiaohongshu
- Paginated publish history, filterable by platform/account
- Generation records are linked with publish status to avoid duplicate publishing

### Automated Hosting

- Create scheduled tasks: daily / one-time, number of articles per success, start time + interval
- Content sources: My Materials / Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Hits
- Supports articles and image posts (image posts can be published to WeChat Official Accounts / Xiaohongshu); WeChat Official Accounts require selecting a specific account
- Used materials are automatically marked to prevent reuse
- Real-time task execution logs with automatic retry on failure

### Personal Center

- Clear view of authorization status, plan, expiration date, and credit balance
- Plan purchase / renewal via WeChat QR code payment, effective immediately under the same authorization
- Credit records: AI usage deduction history
- Order records: purchase/renewal orders and payment status
- Software updates: automatic detection of new versions with background download and installation
- Customer service WeChat / QR code

### Security and Privacy

- Account cookies / login states are stored only on the local machine
- AI gateway calls transmit only generation content and do not collect personal data
- The installer does not bundle any API keys

## English Use Cases

- Individual bloggers / self-media teams producing articles in bulk for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijia
- Agency teams managing multiple platform accounts centrally and generating/publishing content on schedule
- Xiaohongshu image-post creation and one-click publishing (cover + content cards)
