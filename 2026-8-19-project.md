## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，面向个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者，将「选题素材 → AI 写作 → 模板排版 → 多平台发布」整合为一条流水线。

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，**内容 100% 过 AI 检测**。账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

### 资源管理

- **今日热点**：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作。
- **微信爆文**：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊。
- **图片素材**：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图。
- **我的图片**：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签。
- **我的素材**：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用。
- 素材完全自定义：用户可自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改；可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定。

### 创作工坊

**文章模式**

- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版。
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测。
- 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器和自定义模板（另存为我的模板）。
- 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用。
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择。
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义。
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件。
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台。

**贴图模式**

- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题。
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片。
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类。
- **自定义模板（高级版）**：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用。
- 贴图素材自定义：可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库。
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题。

### 账号中心

- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存。
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号。
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存。
- 一键前往平台后台。

### 内容发布

- 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号（联动），支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号支持保存草稿箱，贴图支持发布到小红书。
- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选。
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布。

### 自动托管

- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔。
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文。
- 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号。
- 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗。
- 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试。

### 个人中心

- 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然。
- 套餐购买 / 续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）。
- 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水。
- 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态。
- 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装。
- 客服微信 / 二维码。

## 中文使用场景

- **没有灵感、不知道写什么**：今日热点、微信爆文实时聚合，选题库现成可用；我的素材支持导入和 AI 生成，按账号定位批量产出选题大纲；一条主题/素材，AI 直接生成标题、大纲、正文，不再对着空白文档发呆。
- **不会打造账号人设**：AI 生成素材时输入「账号定位/主题」，大模型按人设产出内容；同一素材组的历史大纲会回传给大模型，保持人设连续、避免重复；素材可编辑、可沉淀，形成账号自己的选题库和内容资产。
- **配图没有准确标签**：图片素材由平台 AI 自动打标，不需要手工分类；自动配图按文章内容和标签匹配，选图更贴题；个人图库支持上传图片并自定义标签，标签即筛选用。
- **排版费时间、发布繁琐**：内置多套排版模板 + 自定义模板，一键套用；账号中心统一管理多平台登录态，内容发布先选平台再选账号；自动托管按计划批量生成并发布，素材用完自动标记不重复。
- **内容一眼 AI 味**：文章成品自然、口语化，无模板腔，符合真人创作习惯；内容 100% 过 AI 检测。

## 中文使用方式

1. 在「资源管理」中汇集选题素材：浏览今日热点 / 微信爆文，或导入 TXT / AI 生成「我的素材」。
2. 进入「创作工坊」，选择文章模式或贴图模式，输入主题或选择素材，由 AI 生成标题、大纲、正文或贴图分段。
3. 选择排版模板或首图/内容卡模板，按需自定义配色、字体、CSS 或文字区域，另存为我的模板。
4. 在「账号中心」登录并管理公众号、头条号、百家号、小红书等平台账号。
5. 通过「内容发布」将作品发布到目标平台，或在「自动托管」中创建定时任务由系统按计划自动生成并发布。
6. 在「个人中心」查看授权、积分、订单，必要时进行续费或更新软件。

## English Overview

WanGaoHui is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It covers content scenarios on WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijiahao, and Xiaohongshu (image-post), and integrates the full pipeline: topic sourcing → AI writing → template layout → multi-platform publishing. It is designed for individual bloggers, operations teams, and content creators. Generated articles read naturally with no obvious AI traces, and content passes AI detection 100%. Account credentials and creative data are stored locally and are not transmitted to third parties.

---

## English Features

### Resource Management

- **Today's Hot Topics**: Real-time hot articles for topic selection and secondary creation.
- **WeChat Popular Articles**: Aggregation of popular articles in the WeChat ecosystem, can be taken into the Creation Workshop in one click.
- **Image Assets**: Platform automatically collects image assets and applies AI tagging, with tag filtering and search; tagged images can be used as article illustrations.
- **My Images**: Personal image library, supports uploading images and defining custom tags.
- **My Materials**: Supports importing TXT materials and AI batch generation of outlines based on account positioning; materials are editable and available for automated scheduling.
- Fully customizable materials: users can create material groups, batch import, AI-generate, and edit entries one by one; can be combined with specified layout / cover templates, and bound directly to scheduled tasks.

### Creation Workshop

**Article Mode**

- Input a topic or material, and AI automatically generates: viral title → outline → full article → layout.
- Output has a natural, human style with no AI traces and passes AI detection 100%.
- Multiple built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates, theme generator, and custom templates (save as My Templates).
- Fully customizable layouts: modify color, font, font size, and style parameters on any built-in template, or write custom CSS for fine-tuning; save as "My Templates" for usage equal to built-in templates.
- Auto image matching from the image asset library based on article content, or pick from the personal image library.
- Word count options: 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom.
- Rich-text editing: titles, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTAs, and more.
- Mobile preview, and copy HTML directly to the WeChat Official Account backend.

**Image-Post Mode**

- AI generates image-post titles, segmented bodies, and hashtags from a topic.
- Cover template + content-card template: title rendered onto the cover, body rendered segment by segment into cards.
- 50+ built-in cover templates, categorized by style.
- **Custom Templates (Advanced)**: upload background images, configure text regions (position / size / color / alignment / lines), custom CSS; save as My Templates for use across all endpoints.
- Customizable image-post assets: upload personal cover / content-card background images, freely define title and body text regions, and save into a personal template library.
- Image posts published to WeChat Official Accounts automatically include hashtags.

### Account Center

- Login support via QR code or web page for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao (Xiaohongshu supports image-post publishing); cookies are saved automatically.
- WeChat Official Accounts: sync multiple accounts under the same name, mark the currently active account.
- Account on/off switches, originality / first-release / AI-declaration settings persist locally.
- One-click jump to platform backends.

### Content Publishing

- Unified publishing for articles and image posts: select platform first, then account (linked); supports publishing to WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao; WeChat Official Accounts support saving to drafts; image posts support publishing to Xiaohongshu.
- Paginated publishing history, filterable by platform and account.
- Generation records are linked to publishing status to prevent duplicate publishing.

### Automated Scheduling

- Create timed tasks: daily / one-time, number of articles per run, start time and interval.
- Content sources: My Materials / Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Popular Articles.
- Supports articles and image posts (image posts can publish to WeChat Official Accounts / Xiaohongshu); WeChat Official Accounts require selecting a specific account.
- Used materials are auto-marked to avoid reuse.
- Real-time task execution logs; automatic retry on failure.

### Personal Center

- Authorization status, plan, expiration date, and credit balance at a glance.
- Plan purchase / renewal via WeChat QR code payment; takes effect automatically under the same authorization.
- Credit history: AI usage deduction records.
- Order history: purchase / renewal orders and payment status.
- Software updates: auto-detect new versions, background download, automatic install.
- Customer-service WeChat / QR code.

## English Use Cases

- **No inspiration, unsure what to write**: Today's Hot Topics and WeChat Popular Articles are aggregated in real time, ready as a topic library; My Materials support importing and AI generation, producing outlines in batches aligned with the account's positioning; from a single topic or material, AI directly generates title, outline, and body.
- **Difficulty building a consistent account persona**: When AI generates materials, input "account positioning / topic" and the model produces content aligned to that persona; historical outlines of the same material group are fed back to the model to maintain persona continuity and avoid repetition; materials are editable and accumulate into the account's own topic and content assets.
- **Image assets lack accurate tags**: Image assets are auto-tagged by the platform's AI without manual classification; auto image matching selects images by article content and tags; the personal image library supports uploads with custom tags used directly for filtering.
- **Layout is time-consuming and publishing is cumbersome**: Multiple built-in layout templates plus custom templates apply with one click; the Account Center centrally manages logins across platforms, and content publishing requires selecting the platform first, then the account; automated scheduling generates and publishes in batches per plan, with materials auto-marked when used up to prevent reuse.
- **Content obviously looks AI-generated**: Articles read naturally and conversationally with no template voice, fitting real creators' habits, and pass AI detection 100%.

## English Usage

1. Gather topic materials in **Resource Management**: browse Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Popular Articles, or import TXT files / AI-generate My Materials.
2. Open the **Creation Workshop**, choose Article Mode or Image-Post Mode, enter a topic or pick materials, and let AI generate the title, outline, body, or image-post segments.
3. Select layout, cover, or content-card templates; customize color, font, CSS, or text regions as needed and save as My Templates.
4. Sign in and manage WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijiahao, Xiaohongshu, and other platform accounts in **Account Center**.
5. Publish through **Content Publishing**, or create timed tasks in **Automated Scheduling** for the system to generate and publish automatically on schedule.
6. Check authorization, credits, and orders in **Personal Center**; renew plans or update the software as needed.
