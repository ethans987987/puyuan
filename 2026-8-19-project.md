## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号、小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者完成选题、AI 写作、模板排版与多平台发布全流程。

产品口号：**选题素材 → AI 写作 → 模板排版 → 多平台发布，一条流水线完成。**

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，**内容 100% 过 AI 检测**。

账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

### 资源管理
- 今日热点：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作
- 微信爆文：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
- 图片素材：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图
- 我的图片：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签
- 我的素材：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用
- 素材完全自定义：用户自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改，素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定

### 创作工坊

**文章模式**
- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测
- 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器、自定义模板（另存为我的模板）
- 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台

**贴图模式**
- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类
- 自定义模板（高级版）：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
- 贴图素材自定义：除了内置模板，用户可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题

### 账号中心
- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存
- 一键前往平台后台

### 内容发布
- 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号（联动），支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号支持保存草稿箱，贴图支持发布到小红书
- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

### 自动托管
- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文
- 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号
- 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗
- 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试

### 个人中心
- 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然
- 套餐购买 / 续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）
- 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水
- 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态
- 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装
- 客服微信 / 二维码

## 中文使用场景

1. 没有灵感、不知道写什么：今日热点、微信爆文实时聚合，选题库现成可用；我的素材支持导入和 AI 生成，按账号定位批量产出选题大纲；一条主题/素材，AI 直接生成标题、大纲、正文。
2. 不会打造账号人设：AI 生成素材时输入「账号定位/主题」，大模型按人设产出内容；同一素材组的历史大纲会回传给大模型，保持人设连续、避免重复；素材可编辑、可沉淀。
3. 配图没有准确标签：图片素材由平台 AI 自动打标，不需要手工分类；自动配图按文章内容和标签匹配；个人图库支持上传图片并自定义标签。
4. 排版费时间、发布繁琐：内置多套排版模板 + 自定义模板，一键套用；账号中心统一管理多平台登录态；自动托管按计划批量生成并发布，素材用完自动标记不重复。
5. 内容一眼 AI 味：文章成品自然、口语化，无模板腔，符合真人创作习惯，内容 100% 过 AI 检测。

## 中文使用方式

- 下载并安装万稿汇桌面客户端
- 在「账号中心」中扫码或网页登录公众号、头条号、百家号（小红书支持贴图发布），账号 Cookie 仅保存在本机
- 在「资源管理」中查看今日热点、微信爆文，或导入/AI 生成「我的素材」

- 在「自动托管」中创建定时任务，绑定素材组与排版/首图模板，按计划批量生成并发布

## English Overview

Wan Gao Hui is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It covers content scenarios on WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijia, and Xiaohongshu (image-card posts), and helps individual bloggers, operations teams, and content creators handle topic selection, AI writing, template-based layout, and multi-platform publishing in one workflow.

Tagline: Topic material → AI writing → Template layout → Multi-platform publishing, completed in a single pipeline.

Generated articles sound natural with no AI traces and pass AI detection 100% of the time. Account credentials and creative data are stored locally and not sent to any third party.

## English Features

### Resource Management
- Today's Hot Topics: Real-time trending articles for topic selection and secondary creation
- WeChat Viral Posts: Aggregated viral posts from the WeChat ecosystem, can be brought into the Creation Workshop in one click
- Image Materials: Platform-collected images with AI auto-tagging, searchable and filterable by tags; tagged images can be used as article illustrations
- My Images: Personal image library supporting uploads and custom tags
- My Materials: Import TXT materials or use AI to generate outlines in batches based on account positioning; materials are editable and feed Scheduled Hosting
- Fully customizable materials: user-defined groups, batch import, AI generation, per-item editing; not limited to text, can be paired with specific layout / cover templates, and bound directly to scheduled tasks

### Creation Workshop

**Article Mode**
- Input topic/material, AI automatically generates: viral title → outline → full body → layout
- Natural, human-like output with no AI traces, 100% passes AI detection
- Built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates, plus theme generator and custom templates (saved as My Templates)
- Fully customizable layout: modify colors, fonts, sizes, style parameters on any built-in template, or write custom CSS, then save as "My Templates" with the same usage rights as built-in templates
- Auto illustration: matches images from the material library by article content, or pick from the personal library
- Word count options: 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
- Rich-text editing components: titles, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTA, etc.
- Mobile preview, and copy HTML to WeChat Official Account editor

**Image-Card Mode**
- AI generates from the topic: card title + segmented body + hashtags
- Cover template + content card template; title rendered onto cover, body rendered segment by segment into cards
- 50+ built-in cover templates, organized by style
- Custom templates (advanced): upload background images, configure text regions (position / font size / color / alignment / line count), add custom CSS, save as My Templates to use across the app
- Custom image-card materials: in addition to built-in templates, users can upload their own cover / content-card backgrounds, freely define text regions for title and body, and store them in a personal template library
- Publish image cards to WeChat Official Account with auto-appended hashtags

### Account Center
- WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, Baijia support QR-code or web login (Xiaohongshu supports image-card publishing); cookies are saved automatically
- WeChat Official Account supports syncing multiple subscribed accounts and marking the active one
- Account switches and persistent configurations such as Original / First-post / AI declaration
- One-click jump to each platform's backend

### Content Publishing
- Unified publishing for articles and image cards: pick platform first → pick account (linked). Supports WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, Baijia; WeChat Official Account supports saving to drafts; image cards support publishing to Xiaohongshu
- Paged publishing history, filterable by platform / account
- Generation records linked with publishing status to avoid duplicate publishing

### Scheduled Hosting
- Create timed tasks: daily / one-time, target article count, start time + interval
- Content sources: My Materials / Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Posts
- Supports articles and image cards (image cards can be published to WeChat Official Account / Xiaohongshu); WeChat Official Account requires selecting a specific account
- Used materials are auto-marked, no duplication
- Real-time task execution logs, with automatic retry on failure

### Personal Center
- Clear overview of authorization status, plan, expiration date, and credit balance
- Plan purchase / renewal: WeChat QR-code payment, automatically effective after payment (same authorization)
- Credit records: AI usage deduction history
- Order records: purchase / renewal orders and payment status
- Software update: auto-detect new versions, background download, auto-install
- Customer support WeChat / QR code

## English Use Cases

1. No inspiration, unsure what to write: today's hot topics and WeChat viral posts aggregated in real time, with a ready-made topic library; My Materials supports import and AI generation to produce outlines in batches based on positioning; one topic / material, AI directly outputs title, outline, and body.
2. Cannot build an account persona: input "account positioning / topic" when AI generates materials, and the model produces content aligned with the persona; historical outlines from the same material group are sent back to the model to keep the persona consistent and avoid repetition; materials are editable and accumulate as account assets.
3. Images lack accurate tags: image materials are auto-tagged by the platform's AI, no manual classification required; auto illustration matches by article content and tags; personal library supports uploads with custom tags.
4. Layout and publishing are time-consuming: built-in templates + custom templates, one-click apply; the Account Center unifies multi-platform login state; Scheduled Hosting generates and publishes in batches on schedule, with used materials auto-marked to prevent reuse.
5. Output reads like AI: articles are natural and conversational, free of template tone, matching real human writing habits, and pass AI detection 100% of the time.

## English Usage

- Download and install the Wan Gao Hui desktop client
- In Account Center, log in to WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, and Baijia via QR code or web login (Xiaohongshu supports image-card publishing); account cookies are stored locally only
- In Resource Management, browse today's hot topics and WeChat viral posts, or import / AI-generate My Materials
- Open Creation Workshop, choose Article Mode or Image-Card Mode, input topic / material, and one-click generate title, outline, body, and layout
- Pick a built-in template or a custom template (with CSS tweaks), and use auto illustration or manual image selection
- In Content Publishing, pick the platform first then the account, publish to WeChat Official Account (save to drafts supported), Toutiao, or Baijia, or publish image cards to Xiaohongshu
- In Scheduled Hosting, create timed tasks, bind material groups and layout / cover templates, and let the system generate and publish in batches on schedule
- In Personal Center, check authorization status, plan, credits, and orders, and renew plans via WeChat QR-code payment
