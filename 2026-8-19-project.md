## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者完成从选题素材、AI 写作、模板排版到多平台发布的一条流水线。

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，内容 100% 过 AI 检测。主要能力包括：从热点/爆文中快速选题；AI 生成文章大纲、正文与排版；一键制作公众号贴图（首图 + 内容卡）；自动/手动配图；多平台账号管理和内容发布；定时自动托管生成并发布内容。

账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

**资源管理**

- 今日热点：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作
- 微信爆文：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
- 图片素材：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图
- 我的图片：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签
- 我的素材：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用
- 素材完全自定义：用户自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改，素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定

**创作工坊 — 文章模式**

- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测
- 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器、自定义模板（另存为我的模板）
- 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台

**创作工坊 — 贴图模式**

- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类
- 自定义模板（高级版）：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
- 贴图素材自定义：除了内置模板，用户可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题

**账号中心**

- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存
- 一键前往平台后台

**内容发布**

- 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号（联动），支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号支持保存草稿箱，贴图支持发布到小红书
- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

**自动托管**

- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文
- 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号
- 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗
- 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试

**个人中心**

- 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然
- 套餐购买 / 续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）
- 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水
- 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态
- 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装
- 客服微信 / 二维码

**安全与隐私**

- 账号 Cookie/登录态仅保存在本机
- AI 网关调用只传递生成内容，不采集个人数据
- 安装包不内置任何 API 密钥

- 排版费时间、发布繁琐：内置多套排版模板 + 自定义模板一键套用；账号中心统一管理多平台登录态；自动托管按计划批量生成并发布

## 中文使用方式

1. 在「账号中心」登录或绑定公众号、头条号、百家号、小红书等平台账号，Cookie 自动保存到本机
2. 在「资源管理」中浏览今日热点或微信爆文挑选选题，导入或 AI 生成「我的素材」
3. 在「创作工坊」选择文章模式或贴图模式，输入主题/素材，让 AI 生成标题、大纲、正文或贴图分段内容
4. 选择内置或自定义排版模板 / 首图模板，配置自动配图，手机预览效果并复制 HTML 或直接发布
5. 在「内容发布」选择平台和账号，一键发布或保存草稿
6. 在「自动托管」创建定时任务，绑定素材组或热点来源，按计划自动批量生成并发布
7. 在「个人中心」查看授权、套餐、积分与订单，进行续费或软件更新

## English Overview

WanGaohui is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It covers content scenarios including WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijiahao, and Xiaohongshu (image posts), helping individual bloggers, agency teams, and content creators complete a one-pipeline workflow: topic selection → AI writing → template-based typesetting → multi-platform publishing.

Articles are natural with no AI flavor, and the content passes AI detection 100%. Core capabilities include fast topic selection from trending/viral posts, AI-generated outlines, body text and typesetting, one-click creation of WeChat image posts (cover + content cards), automatic/manual image matching, multi-platform account management and publishing, and scheduled auto-hosting for generation and publishing.

Account credentials and creative data are stored locally and do not pass through any third party.

## English Features

**Resource Management**

- Today's Trends: real-time trending articles, available for topic selection and derivative creation
- WeChat Viral Posts: aggregated viral posts from the WeChat ecosystem, with one-click handoff to the creation workshop
- Image Materials: platform-collected images with AI tagging, filterable and searchable by tag; tagged images can be used as article illustrations
- My Images: personal image library with upload and custom tagging
- My Materials: supports importing TXT materials or AI batch generating outlines based on account positioning; materials are editable and available for auto-hosting
- Fully customizable materials: users build material groups, batch import, AI-generate, and edit one by one; materials are not limited to text and can be used with specified typesetting templates or cover templates; hosting tasks can be bound directly

**Creation Workshop — Article Mode**

- Input a topic/material, AI auto-generates: viral-style title → outline → full body → typesetting
- Natural human-like output with no AI flavor; passes AI detection 100%
- Built-in WeChat typesetting templates with theme generator and custom templates (save as "My Templates")
- Fully customizable typesetting: modify colors, fonts, font sizes, and style parameters on any built-in template, or apply custom CSS fine-tuning; save as "My Templates" for the same usage as built-in templates
- Auto-matching illustrations: matches from the image library by article content, or selected from the personal image library
- Word count selectable at 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
- Rich text editing: headings, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTA components, etc.
- Mobile preview, supports copying HTML into the WeChat Official Account backend

**Creation Workshop — Image Post Mode**

- AI generates from the topic: image-post title + segmented body + hashtags
- Cover template + content card template; the title renders onto the cover, body segments render as cards
- 50+ built-in cover templates, classified by style
- Custom Template (Advanced): upload background images, configure text regions (position/font size/color/alignment/lines), apply custom CSS; save as "My Templates" for use across all endpoints
- Custom image-post materials: beyond built-in templates, users can upload their own cover/content card backgrounds, freely define text regions for title and body, and save them as a personal template library
- Image posts publish to WeChat Official Accounts with hashtags automatically appended to the body

**Account Center**

- Supports WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, and Baijiahao login via QR code or web page (Xiaohongshu supports image-post publishing); cookies saved automatically
- WeChat Official Accounts supports syncing multiple accounts under the same name, with a marker for the currently active account
- Account toggles, plus Original/First-published/AI declaration settings, are persisted
- One-click access to platform backends

**Content Publishing**

- Unified article/image-post publishing: select platform → select account (linked); supports WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, and Baijiahao publishing; WeChat Official Accounts supports saving to drafts; image posts support publishing to Xiaohongshu
- Paginated publishing history, filterable by platform/account
- Generation records linked with publishing status to avoid duplicate publishing

**Auto-Hosting**

- Create scheduled tasks: daily/one-time, number of successful items per run, start time + interval
- Content sources: My Materials / Today's Trends / WeChat Viral Posts
- Supports articles and image posts (image posts can publish to WeChat Official Accounts / Xiaohongshu); WeChat Official Accounts requires selecting a specific account
- Used materials are auto-marked to avoid repeated consumption
- Real-time task execution logs; automatic retry on failure

**Personal Center**

- Authorization status, plan, expiration time, and credit balance at a glance
- Plan purchase/renewal: WeChat QR-code payment, automatically effective after payment (same authorization)
- Credit records: AI usage deduction history
- Order records: purchase/renewal orders and payment status
- Software update: auto-detect new versions, background download, automatic installation
- Customer service WeChat / QR code

**Security and Privacy**

- Account cookies/login states are saved locally only
- AI gateway calls transmit generated content only; no personal data is collected
- The installer contains no embedded API keys

## English Use Cases

- No inspiration, unsure what to write: Today's Trends and WeChat Viral Posts aggregate in real time with ready-to-use topics; My Materials supports import and AI generation for batch outlines based on account positioning; one topic/material directly generates a title, outline, and body
- Difficulty building an account persona: input "account positioning/topic" when AI-generates materials so the model produces on-persona content; historical outlines from the same material group are sent back to the model to maintain persona continuity and avoid repetition; materials are editable and can be accumulated
- Illustrations without accurate tags: platform AI auto-tags image materials; auto-matched illustrations align with content and tags; personal image library supports custom tags used as filters
- Time-consuming typesetting and cumbersome publishing: built-in typesetting templates plus custom templates applied with one click; Account Center centrally manages multi-platform login states; Auto-Hosting generates and publishes in batches on a schedule
- Content that obviously reads as AI: output is natural and conversational without a templated tone, and the content passes AI detection 100%

## English Usage

1. In Account Center, log in to or bind WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, Baijiahao, Xiaohongshu, etc.; cookies are saved locally
2. In Resource Management, browse Today's Trends or WeChat Viral Posts to pick topics, import or AI-generate My Materials
3. In Creation Workshop, choose Article Mode or Image Post Mode, enter a topic/material, and let AI generate the title, outline, body, or segmented image-post content
4. Select built-in or custom typesetting/cover templates, configure auto-matched illustrations, preview the mobile result, and copy the HTML or publish directly
5. In Content Publishing, select a platform and account to publish with one click or save as a draft
6. In Auto-Hosting, create scheduled tasks, bind a material group or trend source, and run batch generation and publishing on a schedule
7. In Personal Center, view authorization, plan, credits, and orders; renew or update the software
