## 项目介绍

万稿汇是一款**多平台内容生产桌面工具**，以「选题素材 → AI 写作 → 模板排版 → 多平台发布」为一条完整流水线，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，服务于个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者。

产品核心定位是：让 AI 生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，**内容 100% 过 AI 检测**。同时坚持账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

---

## 中文介绍

万稿汇围绕内容生产全流程，从选题、写作、排版、配图，到多平台账号管理和自动托管发布，提供一体化桌面工具。生成的文章呈现自然口语化风格，符合真人创作习惯，无模板腔，并通过 AI 检测。

工作流总览：**今日热点 / 微信爆文 / 我的素材 → AI 生成标题、大纲、正文 → 模板排版 → 自动/手动配图 → 多平台发布 / 自动托管**。

---

## 中文核心能力

- **资源管理**
  - 今日热点：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作
  - 微信爆文：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
  - 图片素材：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索
  - 我的图片：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签
  - 我的素材：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，可自建素材组、批量导入，逐条编辑修改
  - 素材完全自定义：素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定

- **创作工坊**
  - **文章模式**：输入主题或素材，AI 自动生成爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版；成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测
  - 文章排版自定义：内置多套公众号排版模板，可基于任意模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」与内置模板同等使用
  - 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容和标签匹配，或从个人图库选择；字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
  - 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件；支持手机效果预览，复制 HTML 到公众号后台
  - **贴图模式**：AI 根据主题生成贴图标题、分段正文与话题；首图模板渲染标题，内容卡模板逐段渲染正文
  - 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类；自定义模板（高级版）支持上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
  - 贴图素材自定义：可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库

- **账号中心**
  - 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码或网页登录，小红书支持贴图发布，Cookie 自动保存在本机
  - 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
  - 账号开关、原创 / 首发 / AI 声明等配置持久化保存；支持一键前往平台后台

- **内容发布**
  - 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号，支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号可保存草稿箱，贴图可发布到小红书
  - 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
  - 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

- **自动托管**
  - 创建定时任务：每天或一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
  - 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文
  - 支持文章和贴图，贴图可发布到公众号/小红书，公众号需选择具体账号
  - 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗；任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试

- **个人中心**
  - 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然
  - 套餐购买/续费支持微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）
  - 积分记录展示 AI 使用扣费流水；订单记录展示购买/续费订单及支付状态
  - 软件支持自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装
  - 内置客服微信和二维码

## English Overview

WanGaoHui is a multi-platform desktop content production tool that delivers an end-to-end pipeline: topic sourcing → AI writing → templated layout → multi-platform publishing. It covers content scenarios for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijia, and Xiaohongshu (image posts), and serves individual bloggers, agency teams, and content creators.

The product focuses on AI-generated articles that read naturally and leave no AI traces, with **100% pass rate on AI content detection**. Account credentials and creation data are stored locally on the user's machine and are not transmitted to third parties.

---

## English Features

- **Resource Management**
  - Today's Hot Topics: Real-time aggregated trending articles for topic selection and secondary creation
  - WeChat Viral Posts: Aggregated viral posts from the WeChat ecosystem, one-click import to the Creation Studio
  - Image Library: Platform-collected image assets with automatic AI tagging, filterable and searchable by tag
  - My Images: Personal image library supporting image upload and custom tags
  - My Materials: Import TXT materials, or use AI to batch-generate outlines based on account positioning; materials are editable, customizable into groups, bulk-importable, and editable entry by entry
  - Fully customizable materials: Not limited to text; can be paired with specific layout templates / cover templates, and directly bound to scheduled tasks

- **Creation Studio**
  - **Article Mode**: Input a topic or material; AI automatically generates a viral-style title → outline → full article → layout. Output is naturally written, shows no AI traces, and passes AI detection 100%
  - Fully customizable article layout: Built-in WeChat Official Account templates; modify colors, fonts, font sizes, style parameters based on any template, or write custom CSS for fine-tuning. Save as "My Templates" with the same usage as built-in templates
  - Auto illustration: Match images from the Image Library by article content and tags, or select from My Images. Word count options of 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
  - Rich text editing: Components include titles, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTA, etc. Mobile preview and copy HTML to WeChat Official Account backend
  - **Image Post Mode**: AI generates an image-post title, segmented body, and topics based on the topic. Cover template renders the title; content card templates render each segment of the body
  - Over 50 built-in cover templates organized by style. The Custom Template (Advanced) supports uploading background images, configuring text areas (position/font size/color/alignment/lines), and custom CSS; saved as My Templates available across the product
  - Customizable image-post materials: Upload your own cover / content card background images, freely define text areas for titles and body, and save them into your personal template library

- **Account Center**
  - QR code or web login supported for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijia. Xiaohongshu supports image-post publishing. Cookies are saved automatically on the local machine
  - WeChat Official Accounts support syncing multiple accounts under one name and marking the currently active account
  - Account switches, originality / first-publish / AI declaration settings are persisted
  - One-click access to platform backends

- **Content Publishing**
  - Unified publishing for articles and image posts: select platform first, then account. Supports publishing to WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijia. WeChat Official Accounts support saving to drafts. Image posts support publishing to Xiaohongshu
  - Publishing history with pagination; filterable by platform / account
  - Generation records and publishing status are linked to avoid duplicate publishing

- **Automated Scheduling**
  - Create scheduled tasks: daily or one-time, number of successful pieces per run, start time + interval
  - Content sources: My Materials / Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Posts
  - Supports articles and image posts; image posts can be published to WeChat Official Accounts / Xiaohongshu; WeChat Official Accounts require selecting a specific account
  - Used materials are automatically flagged to avoid reuse
  - Task execution logs are viewable in real time; failed runs are automatically retried

- **Personal Center**
  - License status, plan, expiration date, and credit balance at a glance
  - Plan purchase / renewal via WeChat QR code payment, automatically takes effect under the same license
  - Credit records show AI usage deduction history; order records show purchase/renewal orders and payment status
  - Automatic new-version detection with background download and installation
  - Built-in customer service WeChat and QR code
