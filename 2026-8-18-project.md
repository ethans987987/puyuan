## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，围绕「选题素材 → AI 写作 → 模板排版 → 多平台发布」一条流水线完成，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号、小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者高效产出可发布的内容。

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，内容 100% 过 AI 检测。账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

### 资源管理

- **今日热点**：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作。
- **微信爆文**：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊。
- **图片素材**：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图。
- **我的图片**：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签。
- **我的素材**：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用。
- 素材完全自定义：用户自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改，素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定。

### 创作工坊

**文章模式**

- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版。
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测。
- 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器、自定义模板（另存为我的模板）。
- 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用。
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择。
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义。
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件。
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台。

**贴图模式**

- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题。
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片。
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类。
- **自定义模板（高级版）**：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用。
- 贴图素材自定义：除了内置模板，用户可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库。
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题。

### 账号中心

- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存。
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号。
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存。
- 一键前往平台后台。

### 内容发布

- 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号（联动），支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号支持保存草稿箱，贴图支持发布到小红书。
- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选。
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布。

### 自动托管

- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔。
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文。
- 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号。
- 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗。
- 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试。

### 个人中心

- 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然。
- 套餐购买 / 续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）。
- 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水。
- 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态。
- 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装。
- 客服微信 / 二维码。

## 中文使用场景

- 个人博主：不知道写什么时，从今日热点 / 微信爆文快速选题，AI 直接生成标题、大纲与正文，一键套用模板排版并发布。
- 代运营团队：在账号中心统一管理多平台账号与登录态，按账号定位沉淀素材库，自动托管按计划批量产出和发布。
- 公众号运营：用文章模式生成可过 AI 检测的自然内容，或用贴图模式一键制作首图 + 内容卡组合发布。
- 多平台分发：同一内容流水线发布到公众号、头条号、百家号，或将贴图发布到小红书，减少重复劳动。
- 账号人设打造：在 AI 生成素材时输入「账号定位/主题」，自动保持人设连续与内容不重复。

## 中文使用方式

1. 安装桌面客户端，登录后在「账号中心」扫码或网页登录公众号 / 头条号 / 百家号账号，小红书支持贴图发布。
2. 在「资源管理」中选择「今日热点」「微信爆文」或自建「我的素材」，也可上传图片到「我的图片」并打标签。
3. 进入「创作工坊」，选择「文章模式」或「贴图模式」，输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成标题、大纲、正文与排版。
4. 套用内置模板或自定义模板，预览手机效果，复制 HTML 或直接发布。
5. 在「内容发布」中选择平台与账号，一键发布或在公众号保存为草稿箱。
6. 在「自动托管」中创建定时任务，绑定素材来源与文章/贴图模式，按计划自动生成并发布，素材已使用自动标记。
7. 在「个人中心」查看授权、套餐、积分与订单，管理软件更新与客服联系。

## English Overview

Wan Gao Hui (万稿汇) is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It completes the full pipeline of topic & material gathering → AI writing → template layout → multi-platform publishing in one workflow, covering content scenarios such as WeChat Official Accounts (公众号), Toutiao (头条号), Baijiahao (百家号), and Xiaohongshu (小红书, image cards). It is designed for individual bloggers, agency operations teams, and content creators.

The generated articles read naturally with no AI traces, and the content passes AI detection at a 100% rate. Account credentials and creative data are stored locally and are not transmitted to third parties.

## English Features

### Resource Management

- **Today's Hot Topics (今日热点)**: real-time hot articles, available for topic selection and secondary creation.
- **WeChat Viral Articles (微信爆文)**: aggregated viral articles from the WeChat ecosystem, one-click transfer into the creation workshop.
- **Image Materials (图片素材)**: the platform automatically collects image materials and applies AI tagging, with tag-based filtering and search; tagged images can be used as article illustrations.
- **My Images (我的图片)**: a personal image library supporting image uploads and custom tags.
- **My Materials (我的素材)**: supports importing TXT materials and AI batch generation of content outlines based on account positioning; materials are editable and available for automated hosting.
- Fully customizable materials: users can build their own material groups, batch-import, AI-generate, and edit entries one by one. Materials are not limited to text, can be paired with designated layout templates / cover-image templates, and can be directly bound to hosting tasks.

### Creation Workshop

**Article Mode**

- Input a topic / material, and AI automatically generates: viral title → article outline → full body → layout.
- The output reads naturally in a human style with no AI traces, passing AI detection at a 100% rate.
- Built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates, with a theme generator and custom templates (save as "My Templates").
- Fully customizable layout: modify color, font, font size, and style parameters based on any built-in template, or write custom CSS for fine adjustments, and save as "My Templates" for equal use with built-in templates.
- Auto illustration: matching from the image material library based on article content, or selecting from the personal image library.
- Word count selectable at 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom.
- Rich-text editing: headings, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTAs and other components.
- Mobile preview, with HTML copy-to-clipboard support for the WeChat Official Account backend.

**Image Card Mode (贴图模式)**

- AI generates based on the topic: image card title + segmented body + hashtags.
- Cover-image template + content card template: the title is rendered on the cover image, and the body is segmented into cards.
- 50+ built-in cover-image templates, classified by style.
- **Custom Templates (Advanced)**: upload background images, configure text areas (position / font size / color / alignment / line count), and custom CSS, save as "My Templates" for use across all platforms.
- Image card materials are customizable: in addition to built-in templates, users can upload their own cover-image / content card backgrounds, freely define the text areas for the title and body, and save them to the personal template library.
- Image cards published to the WeChat Official Account automatically include hashtags.

### Account Center

- Supports QR code / web login for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao (Xiaohongshu supports image card publishing), with automatic cookie saving.
- WeChat Official Accounts support syncing multiple accounts under the same name, with the current active account marked.
- Account toggles, originality / first-release / AI declaration and other configurations are persistently saved.
- One-click redirect to the platform backend.

### Content Publishing

- Unified publishing for articles / image cards: select platform first → then select account (linked). Supports publishing to WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao; WeChat Official Accounts support saving to drafts; image cards support publishing to Xiaohongshu.
- Paginated publishing history, filterable by platform / account.
- Generation records are linked with publishing status to avoid duplicate publishing.

### Automated Hosting

- Create timed tasks: daily / one-time, number of successful pieces per run, start time + interval.
- Content sources: My Materials / Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Articles.
- Supports articles and image cards (image cards can be published to WeChat Official Accounts / Xiaohongshu), and a specific WeChat Official Account must be selected.
- Used materials are automatically marked to avoid repeated consumption.
- Real-time task execution logs, with automatic retry on failure.

### Personal Center

- Authorization status, plan, expiration date, and credit balance at a glance.
- Plan purchase / renewal: WeChat QR code payment, automatically activated after payment (under the same authorization).
- Credit records: AI usage deduction history.
- Order records: purchase / renewal orders and payment status.
- Software update: automatically detects new versions, downloads in background, and installs automatically.
- Customer service WeChat / QR code.

## English Use Cases

- **Individual bloggers**: when out of ideas, quickly pick topics from Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Articles, let AI generate the title, outline, and body, then apply templates for layout and publishing in one step.
- **Agency operations teams**: centrally manage multi-platform accounts and login states in the Account Center, build a material library based on account positioning, and let Automated Hosting batch-produce and publish content on schedule.
- **WeChat Official Account operators**: use Article Mode to generate natural content that passes AI detection, or use Image Card Mode to produce cover-image + content card combinations in one click.
- **Multi-platform distribution**: publish the same content pipeline to WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao, or publish image cards to Xiaohongshu, reducing repeated work.
- **Account persona building**: input the "account positioning / topic" when AI-generates materials, automatically maintaining persona continuity and avoiding content repetition.

## English Usage

1. Install the desktop client and log in. In the Account Center, log in to WeChat Official Accounts / Toutiao / Baijiahao via QR code or web login. Xiaohongshu supports image card publishing.
2. In Resource Management, choose Today's Hot Topics, WeChat Viral Articles, or build your own My Materials. You can also upload images to My Images and tag them.
3. Enter the Creation Workshop and choose Article Mode or Image Card Mode. Input the topic / material, and AI will automatically generate the title, outline, body, and layout.
4. Apply a built-in template or a custom template, preview the mobile view, copy the HTML, or publish directly.
5. In Content Publishing, select the platform and account, then publish in one step or save as drafts in the WeChat Official Account.
6. In Automated Hosting, create a timed task, bind the material source and the article / image card mode, and let content be generated and published on schedule. Used materials are automatically marked.
7. In the Personal Center, view authorization, plans, credits, and orders, and manage software updates and customer service contact.

## Security & Privacy

- Account cookies / login states are stored locally only.
- AI gateway calls only transmit generation content and do not collect personal data.
- The installer does not embed any API keys.
