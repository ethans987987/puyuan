## 中文

### 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者完成「选题素材 → AI 写作 → 模板排版 → 多平台发布」一条流水线。

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，**内容 100% 过 AI 检测**。

账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

### 中文核心能力

**资源管理**
- 今日热点：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作
- 微信爆文：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
- 图片素材：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图
- 我的图片：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签
- 我的素材：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用
- 素材完全自定义：用户可自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改，素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定

**创作工坊 - 文章模式**
- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测
- 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器、自定义模板（另存为我的模板）
- 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台

**创作工坊 - 贴图模式**
- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类
- 自定义模板（高级版）：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
- 贴图素材自定义：除了内置模板，用户可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题

**账号中心**
- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存
- 一键前往平台后台

**内容发布**
- 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号（联动），支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号支持保存草稿箱，贴图支持发布到小红书
- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

**自动托管**
- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文
- 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号
- 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗
- 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试

**个人中心**
- 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然
- 套餐购买 / 续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）
- 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水
- 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态
- 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装
- 客服微信 / 二维码

**安全与隐私**
- 账号 Cookie / 登录态仅保存在本机
- AI 网关调用只传递生成内容，不采集个人数据
- 安装包不内置任何 API 密钥

### 中文使用场景

1. **没有灵感、不知道写什么**：今日热点、微信爆文实时聚合，选题库现成可用；我的素材支持导入和 AI 生成，按账号定位批量产出选题大纲；一条主题/素材，AI 直接生成标题、大纲、正文。
2. **不会打造账号人设**：AI 生成素材时可输入「账号定位/主题」，大模型按人设产出内容；同一素材组的历史大纲会回传给大模型，保持人设连续、避免重复；素材可编辑、可沉淀，形成账号自己的选题库和内容资产。
3. **配图没有准确标签**：图片素材由平台 AI 自动打标，不需要手工分类；自动配图按文章内容和标签匹配，选图更贴题；个人图库支持上传图片并自定义标签，标签即筛选用。
4. **排版费时间、发布繁琐**：内置多套排版模板 + 自定义模板，一键套用；账号中心统一管理多平台登录态，内容发布先选平台再选账号；自动托管按计划批量生成并发布，素材用完自动标记不重复。
5. **内容一眼 AI 味**：文章成品自然、口语化，无模板腔，符合真人创作习惯；内容 100% 过 AI 检测。

### 中文使用方式

1. 访问官网 https://toolsoul.daniu7.cn/item/gzh-wgh?site=platform 下载桌面端并安装。
2. 在「账号中心」中扫码登录公众号、头条号、百家号或小红书（贴图发布）账号，Cookie 自动保存。
3. 在「资源管理」中选择今日热点、微信爆文、我的素材或我的图片，准备创作素材。
4. 进入「创作工坊」，选择文章模式或贴图模式，输入主题/素材，AI 生成标题、大纲、正文与排版；可套用内置模板或自定义模板。
5. 在「内容发布」中选择平台与账号，一键发布文章或贴图；公众号可保存草稿箱，贴图可发布至小红书。
6. 在「自动托管」中创建定时任务，按计划批量生成并发布内容，素材用完自动标记。
7. 在「个人中心」查看授权、套餐、积分、订单，或进行更新检测。

## English

### English Overview

WanGaoHui is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It covers content scenarios such as WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijiahao, and Xiaohongshu (image card posts), helping individual bloggers, content operations teams, and content creators complete the pipeline: topic sourcing → AI writing → template layout → multi-platform publishing.

Generated articles read naturally with no AI flavor, **100% passing AI detection**.

Account credentials and creative data are stored locally and are not sent to third parties.

### English Features

**Resource Management**
- Today's Hot Topics: Real-time aggregated trending articles, usable for topic selection and secondary creation
- WeChat Viral Articles: Viral content aggregated from the WeChat ecosystem, can be brought into the Creation Studio in one click
- Image Assets: Platform-collected images automatically tagged by AI, filterable and searchable by tags; tagged images can serve as article illustrations
- My Images: Personal image library, supports uploading images and custom tags
- My Materials: Import TXT materials or let AI batch-generate content outlines by account positioning; materials are editable and usable by scheduled hosting
- Fully custom materials: Build your own material groups, bulk import, AI generation, per-item editing; materials go beyond text, can be paired with specified layout or cover templates, and bound directly to hosting tasks

**Creation Studio – Article Mode**
- Enter a topic/material, AI auto-generates: viral title → article outline → full body → layout
- Natural human-style output with no AI traces, 100% passing AI detection
- Built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates, with theme generator and custom templates (save as "My Templates")
- Fully custom layout: Modify colors, fonts, sizes, and style parameters based on any built-in template, or write custom CSS to fine-tune; save as "My Templates" and use them like built-in ones
- Auto illustrations: Matched from the image asset library by article content, or chosen from your personal library
- Word count: 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
- Rich text editing: Headings, blockquotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTAs, etc.
- Mobile preview; copy HTML into the WeChat Official Account editor

**Creation Studio – Image Card Mode**
- AI generates based on the topic: image card title + segmented body + hashtags
- Cover template + content card template; title rendered onto the cover, body rendered as cards paragraph by paragraph
- 50+ built-in cover templates, categorized by style
- Custom templates (advanced): Upload background images, configure text regions (position / font size / color / alignment / line count) and custom CSS; save as "My Templates" for cross-platform use
- Custom image card materials: Beyond built-in templates, users can upload their own cover/content card backgrounds, freely define title and body text regions, and save them to a personal template library
- Publishing image cards to WeChat Official Accounts auto-appends hashtags

**Account Center**
- Supports QR code / web login for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao (Xiaohongshu supports image-card publishing); cookies are auto-saved
- WeChat Official Accounts: Sync all public accounts under your name and mark the currently active account
- Account switches, originality / first-release / AI declaration settings are persisted
- One-click access to each platform's backend

**Content Publishing**
- Unified publishing for articles/image cards: Pick a platform first → then an account (linked); supports publishing to WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao; WeChat Official Accounts support saving to drafts; image cards support publishing to Xiaohongshu
- Publishing history with pagination, filterable by platform/account
- Generation records linked to publishing status to avoid duplicate publishing

**Scheduled Hosting**
- Create timed tasks: daily / one-time, articles per run, start time + interval
- Content sources: My Materials / Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Articles
- Supports articles and image cards (image cards can be published to WeChat Official Accounts / Xiaohongshu); WeChat Official Accounts require a specific account selection
- Used materials are auto-marked, no repeat consumption
- Real-time task execution logs; automatic retry on failure

**Personal Center**
- Authorization status, plan, expiration date, and credit balance at a glance
- Plan purchase / renewal: WeChat scan-code payment, auto-effective after payment (same authorization)
- Credit records: AI usage deduction history
- Order records: Purchase / renewal orders and payment statuses
- Software updates: Auto-detect new versions, background download, automatic installation
- Customer service WeChat / QR code

**Security & Privacy**
- Account cookies / login states are stored locally only
- AI gateway calls only transmit generated content, no personal data is collected
- The installer does not embed any API keys

### English Use Cases

1. **No inspiration / don't know what to write**: Real-time aggregation of today's hot topics and WeChat viral articles provides a ready-to-use topic library; My Materials supports import and AI generation to batch-produce outlines by account positioning; a single topic/material is enough for AI to directly generate title, outline, and body.
2. **Unable to build an account persona**: Input "account positioning / topic" when AI generates materials and the model produces content according to the persona; historical outlines from the same material group are sent back to the model to keep the persona consistent and avoid repetition; materials are editable and accumulate into the account's own topic library and content assets.
3. **Images lack accurate tags**: Platform AI auto-tags image assets without manual classification; auto illustration matching is based on article content and tags, making images more on-topic; personal image library supports uploading images with custom tags, and tags are used for filtering.
4. **Time-consuming layout and cumbersome publishing**: Built-in multiple layout templates + custom templates for one-click application; Account Center unifies multi-platform login states, and publishing picks platform first then account; Scheduled Hosting batch-generates and publishes per plan, with used materials auto-marked.
5. **Content that looks AI-generated at a glance**: Articles read naturally and conversationally without template tone, matching real-human creation habits; 100% passing AI detection.

### English Usage

1. Visit the official website https://toolsoul.daniu7.cn/item/gzh-wgh?site=platform to download and install the desktop client.
2. In "Account Center", scan to log in to WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, Baijiahao, or Xiaohongshu (image card publishing); cookies are auto-saved.
3. In "Resource Management", choose today's hot topics, WeChat viral articles, My Materials, or My Images to prepare creative materials.
4. Go to "Creation Studio", choose Article Mode or Image Card Mode, enter a topic/material, and let AI generate the title, outline, body, and layout; apply a built-in or custom template.
