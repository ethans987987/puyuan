## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款面向 AI 内容创作与多平台发布的桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者完成选题、写作、排版与多平台发布。生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，内容 100% 过 AI 检测。账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

### 资源管理
- 今日热点、微信爆文实时聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
- 图片素材由平台 AI 自动打标，支持按标签筛选与搜索；个人图库支持上传图片并自定义标签
- 我的素材支持导入 TXT，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑、可沉淀
- 用户可自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑，素材可绑定指定的排版模板/首图模板，供自动托管直接调用

### 创作工坊：文章模式
- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，内容 100% 过 AI 检测
- 内置多套公众号排版模板；支持配色、字体、字号、样式参数修改，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」与内置模板同等使用
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台

### 创作工坊：贴图模式
- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类
- 自定义模板（高级版）：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
- 贴图素材自定义：用户可上传首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存为个人模板
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题

### 账号中心
- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录；小红书支持贴图发布；Cookie 自动保存
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存
- 一键前往平台后台

### 内容发布
- 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号，支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布；公众号支持保存草稿箱；贴图支持发布到小红书
- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

### 自动托管
- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文
- 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号
- 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗
- 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试

### 人设与内容质量
- AI 生成素材时输入「账号定位/主题」，大模型按人设产出内容
- 同一素材组的历史大纲会回传给大模型，保持人设连续、避免重复

### 个人中心
- 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然
- 套餐购买 / 续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）
- 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水
- 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态
- 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装

### 安全与隐私
- 账号 Cookie/登录态仅保存在本机
- AI 网关调用只传递生成内容，不采集个人数据
- 安装包不内置任何 API 密钥

## English Overview

WanGaoHui is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing, covering WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijia, and Xiaohongshu (image posts). It helps individual bloggers, agency teams, and content creators with topic selection, writing, layout, and multi-platform publishing. Generated articles read naturally with no AI traces and pass AI detection at 100%. Account credentials and authoring data are stored locally and never sent to third parties.

## English Features

### Resource Management
- Real-time aggregation of today's trending topics and viral WeChat articles, with one-click import into the creation workshop
- Platform AI auto-tagging for image assets, with tag-based filtering and search; personal image library supports uploads with custom tags
- My Materials supports TXT import and AI batch outline generation based on account positioning; materials are editable and reusable
- User-defined material groups with batch import, AI generation, and per-item editing; materials can be bound to specific layout or cover templates and directly used by auto-scheduling tasks

### Creation Workshop: Article Mode
- Input a topic or material; AI generates viral title → outline → full body → layout
- Articles read naturally with no AI traces and pass AI detection at 100%
- Built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates; customizable color, font, size, and styles, plus custom CSS, savable as personal templates with the same capabilities as built-in templates
- Auto images: matched from the image library by content, or selected from the personal library
- Word count selectable 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
- Rich text editing with titles, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, and CTAs
- Mobile preview, HTML copyable to the WeChat Official Account editor

### Creation Workshop: Image Post Mode
- AI generates image-post title, segmented body, and hashtags from the topic
- Cover template + content card template; title rendered onto cover, body rendered segment-by-segment into cards
- 50+ built-in cover templates, categorized by style
- Advanced custom templates: upload background images, configure text regions (position / size / color / alignment / lines), custom CSS, savable as personal templates for all ends
- Custom image-post assets: users can upload their own cover/card backgrounds and freely define text regions for title and body, saving into a personal template library
- Image posts published to WeChat Official Account with automatic hashtags

### Account Center
- QR code or web login for WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, and Baijia; Xiaohongshu supports image post publishing; cookies saved automatically
- Sync multiple WeChat Official Accounts under the same name, with active account marking
- Persistent settings for account switches, original / first-publish / AI declarations
- One-click access to platform admin panels

### Publishing
- Unified publishing for articles and image posts: choose platform, then account; supports WeChat Official Account, Toutiao, and Baijia; draft saving for WeChat Official Account; image posts support Xiaohongshu
- Paginated publishing history, filterable by platform and account
- Generation records link to publishing status to prevent duplicates

### Auto Scheduling
- Scheduled tasks with daily / one-time mode, articles per run, start time, and interval
- Sources: My Materials / Today's Trending / WeChat Viral Articles
- Supports articles and image posts (image posts may publish to WeChat Official Account / Xiaohongshu); a specific WeChat Official Account must be selected
- Used materials are marked automatically to prevent reuse
- Task execution logs in real time, with automatic retry on failure

### Persona and Content Quality
- Input "account positioning / theme" when generating materials; the model outputs content based on the persona
- Historical outlines from the same material group are fed back to the model to keep persona consistent and avoid repetition

### Personal Center
- Authorization status, plan, expiration, and credit balance at a glance
- Plan purchase / renewal via WeChat QR code payment, auto-activated under the same authorization
- Credit log: AI usage billing entries
- Order history: purchase / renewal orders and payment status
- Software updates: automatic new-version detection with background download and auto-install

### Security and Privacy
- Account cookies and login states are stored only on the local machine
- AI gateway calls transmit only generated content, with no personal data collection
- The installer bundles no API keys

## English Use Cases
- Individual bloggers updating across multiple platforms
- Agency teams producing content in bulk for multiple accounts
- WeChat Official Account articles and image posts
- Toutiao and Baijia article publishing
- Xiaohongshu image post publishing
- Topic selection and derivative content from trending and viral articles
- Scheduled, batch auto-generation and publishing

## English Usage
**Topic materials → AI writing → template layout → multi-platform publishing**, completed on a single pipeline.
