## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者用一条流水线完成：选题素材 → AI 写作 → 模板排版 → 多平台发布。

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，内容 100% 过 AI 检测。账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

### 资源管理
- 今日热点：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作
- 微信爆文：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
- 图片素材：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图
- 我的图片：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签
- 我的素材：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用
- 素材完全自定义：用户自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改，素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板 / 首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定

### 创作工坊 — 文章模式
- 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版
- 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测
- 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器、自定义模板（另存为我的模板）
- 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用
- 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择
- 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
- 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件
- 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台

### 创作工坊 — 贴图模式
- AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题
- 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片
- 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类
- 自定义模板（高级版）：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
- 贴图素材自定义：除了内置模板，用户可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库
- 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题

### 账号中心
- 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存
- 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
- 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存
- 一键前往平台后台

### 内容发布
- 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号（联动），支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号支持保存草稿箱，贴图支持发布到小红书
- 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
- 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

### 自动托管
- 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
- 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文
- 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号
- 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗
- 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试

### 个人中心
- 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然
- 套餐购买/续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）
- 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水
- 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态
- 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装
- 客服微信/二维码

### 安全与隐私
- 账号 Cookie/登录态仅保存在本机
- AI 网关调用只传递生成内容，不采集个人数据
- 安装包不内置任何 API 密钥

## English Overview

Wangaohui (万稿汇) is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It serves personal bloggers, agency operators, and content creators, covering WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, Baijiahao, and Xiaohongshu (image-card posts). The pipeline runs end-to-end: topic sourcing → AI writing → template-based layout → multi-platform publishing.

Generated articles read naturally with no AI traces, and content passes AI detection 100% of the time. Account credentials and authoring data stay on the local machine and are not sent to third parties.

## English Features

### Resource Management
- Today's Hot Topics: real-time trending articles for topic selection and secondary creation
- WeChat Viral Articles: aggregated viral content from the WeChat ecosystem, one-click carry-over to the Creation Workshop
- Image Assets: platform auto-collects and AI-tags images; tag filtering and search supported; tagged images can be used as article illustrations
- My Images: personal image library with custom tags
- My Materials: import TXT materials or generate outlines in bulk via AI based on the account positioning; editable and usable by auto-hosting
- Fully customizable materials: user-built material groups, batch import, AI generation, per-item editing; supports assigned layout templates / cover templates and direct binding to auto-hosting tasks

### Creation Workshop — Article Mode
- Input topic/material, AI auto-generates: viral-style title → outline → full body → layout
- Natural, human-style output with no AI traces; 100% passes AI detection
- Multiple built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates; theme generator and custom templates (save as My Templates)
- Fully customizable layout: tweak colors, fonts, sizes, and style parameters on any built-in template, or write custom CSS; save as My Templates with the same usage rights as built-ins
- Auto image matching from the image asset library by article content, or manual selection from the personal library
- Word count choices: 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
- Rich-text editing: titles, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTA, and more
- Mobile preview and copy HTML to the WeChat Official Account editor

### Creation Workshop — Image-Card Mode
- AI generates image-card title + segmented body + hashtags from the topic
- Cover template + content card template: title rendered onto the cover, body rendered segment by segment into cards
- 50+ built-in cover templates, categorized by style
- Custom Templates (advanced): upload background image, configure text area (position/size/color/alignment/lines), custom CSS; save as My Templates, usable across the board
- Custom image-card assets: beyond built-in templates, users can upload their own cover / content card backgrounds and freely define title and body text areas, saving them to a personal template library
- Publish image cards to WeChat Official Accounts with hashtags auto-appended

### Account Center
- QR code / web login for WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao (Xiaohongshu supports image-card publishing); cookies auto-saved
- WeChat Official Accounts support syncing multiple subscribed accounts and marking the currently active one
- Persistent settings for account switches, originality / first-release / AI disclosure
- One-click jump to each platform's backend

### Content Publishing
- Unified article / image-card publishing: pick platform → pick account (linked); publishing supported to WeChat Official Accounts, Toutiao, and Baijiahao; drafts can be saved to WeChat Official Accounts; image cards can be published to Xiaohongshu
- Paginated publish history, filterable by platform / account
- Generation records linked to publish status to avoid duplicate publishing

### Auto-Hosting
- Create scheduled tasks: daily / one-time, articles per run, start time + interval
- Content sources: My Materials / Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Articles
- Supports articles and image cards (image cards can be published to WeChat Official Accounts / Xiaohongshu); WeChat Official Accounts require a specific account
- Used materials are auto-marked to avoid reuse
- Real-time task execution logs; automatic retry on failure

### Personal Center
- Authorization status, plan, expiration date, and credit balance at a glance
- Plan purchase / renewal: WeChat scan-code payment, auto-activated under the same authorization
- Credit history: AI usage deduction log
- Order history: purchase / renewal orders and payment status
- Software updates: auto-detect new versions, background download and auto-install
- Customer service WeChat / QR code

### Security & Privacy
- Account cookies / login sessions are stored only on the local machine
- AI gateway calls transmit only generation content; no personal data is collected
- The installer ships without any embedded API keys

## English Use Cases

- Quickly pick topics from trending and viral content: real-time aggregation of Today's Hot Topics and WeChat Viral Articles
- Get unstuck without ideas: one topic or material produces title, outline, and body through AI
- Build a consistent account persona: input the account positioning / theme during AI material generation so the model writes in persona; past outlines are fed back to keep the persona consistent and avoid repetition
- Pick accurate images: platform AI auto-tags images; auto image matching is content- and tag-aware
- Manage a personal image library: upload images with custom tags for filtering
- Streamline layout and publishing: built-in templates plus custom templates applied in one click; multi-platform login states managed centrally in the Account Center
- Scheduled auto-hosting: generate and publish in batches on a plan, with used materials auto-marked to prevent repeats
- Remove the AI feel from content: natural, conversational articles that match human writing habits and pass AI detection 100% of the time

## English Usage

1. Download and install the Wangaohui desktop client from the website: https://toolsoul.daniu7.cn/item/gzh-wgh?site=platform
2. In Account Center, sign in to WeChat Official Accounts / Toutiao / Baijiahao via QR code or web login (the same applies to Xiaohongshu for image-card publishing); cookies are auto-saved on the local machine
3. Prepare topics and materials in Resource Management: browse Today's Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Articles, import TXT or AI-generate materials; upload and tag images
4. In Creation Workshop, input a topic or select materials, generate in Article Mode or Image-Card Mode, pick templates and images, preview, then publish
5. In Auto-Hosting, create scheduled tasks by daily / one-time, article count, and time interval; bind the material source and target platform account
6. In Personal Center, manage authorization, plan, credits, orders, and automatic updates
