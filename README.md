## 中文介绍

万稿汇是一款 AI 内容创作与多平台发布桌面工具，覆盖公众号、头条号、百家号和小红书（贴图）等内容场景，帮助个人博主、代运营团队和内容创作者完成从选题素材、AI 写作、模板排版到多平台发布的一条流水线。

生成的文章自然、无 AI 痕迹，**内容 100% 过 AI 检测**。

账号凭据和创作数据保存在本机，不经过第三方。

## 中文核心能力

- **资源管理**
  - 今日热点：实时更新的热点文章，可用于选题和二次创作
  - 微信爆文：微信生态爆文聚合，支持一键带入创作工坊
  - 图片素材：平台自动收集图片素材并进行 AI 打标，支持按标签筛选、搜索；已打标图片可作为文章配图
  - 我的图片：个人图库，支持上传图片并自定义标签
  - 我的素材：支持导入 TXT 素材，也支持 AI 按账号定位批量生成内容大纲；素材可编辑，供自动托管使用
  - 素材完全自定义：用户自建素材组、批量导入、AI 生成、逐条编辑修改，素材不限于文本，可配合指定排版模板/首图模板使用，托管任务可直接绑定

- **创作工坊 · 文章模式**
  - 输入主题/素材，AI 自动生成：爆款标题 → 文章大纲 → 完整正文 → 排版
  - 成品自然真人风格，无 AI 痕迹，100% 过 AI 检测
  - 内置多套公众号排版模板，支持主题生成器、自定义模板（另存为我的模板）
  - 排版完全自定义：基于任意内置模板修改配色、字体、字号、样式参数，或直接写自定义 CSS 微调，另存为「我的模板」后与内置模板同等使用
  - 自动配图：从图片素材库按文章内容匹配，或从个人图库选择
  - 字数可选 800 / 1200 / 1500 或自定义
  - 富文本编辑：标题、引用、卡片、列表、分割线、CTA 等组件
  - 手机效果预览，支持复制 HTML 到公众号后台

- **创作工坊 · 贴图模式**
  - AI 根据主题生成：贴图标题 + 分段正文 + 话题
  - 首图模板 + 内容卡模板，标题渲染到首图、正文逐段渲染成卡片
  - 内置 50+ 首图模板，按风格分类
  - 自定义模板（高级版）：可上传背景图、配置文字区域（位置/字号/颜色/对齐/行数）、自定义 CSS，另存为我的模板后全端通用
  - 贴图素材自定义：除了内置模板，用户可上传自己的首图/内容卡背景图，自由划定标题和正文的文字区域，保存成个人模板库
  - 贴图发布到公众号，正文自动带话题

- **账号中心**
  - 支持公众号、头条号、百家号扫码/网页登录（小红书支持贴图发布），Cookie 自动保存
  - 公众号支持同步名下多个公众号，标记当前生效账号
  - 账号开关、原创/首发/AI 声明等配置持久化保存
  - 一键前往平台后台

- **内容发布**
  - 文章/贴图统一发布：先选平台 → 再选账号（联动），支持公众号、头条号、百家号发布，公众号支持保存草稿箱，贴图支持发布到小红书
  - 发布历史分页，可按平台/账号筛选
  - 生成记录与发布状态联动，避免重复发布

- **自动托管**
  - 创建定时任务：每天/一次、每次成功篇数、开始时间 + 间隔
  - 内容来源：我的素材 / 今日热点 / 微信爆文
  - 支持文章和贴图（贴图可发布到公众号/小红书），公众号需选择具体账号
  - 素材已使用自动标记，不重复消耗
  - 任务执行日志实时查看，失败自动重试

- **个人中心**
  - 授权状态、套餐、到期时间、积分余额一目了然
  - 套餐购买 / 续费：微信扫码支付，支付后自动生效（同一授权）
  - 积分记录：AI 使用扣费流水
  - 订单记录：购买/续费订单及支付状态
  - 软件更新：自动检测新版本，后台下载、自动安装

- **安全与隐私**
  - 账号 Cookie / 登录态仅保存在本机
  - AI 网关调用只传递生成内容，不采集个人数据
  - 安装包不内置任何 API 密钥

## 中文使用方式

1. 在资源管理中浏览「今日热点」「微信爆文」或维护「我的素材」「我的图片」。
2. 进入创作工坊：选择文章模式或贴图模式，输入主题/素材，AI 自动产出标题、大纲、正文与排版；可套用内置模板或自定义模板（含自定义 CSS），并使用自动配图。
3. 在账号中心登录公众号、头条号、百家号等平台账号，Cookie 自动保存在本机。

5. 在自动托管中创建定时任务，绑定素材来源与模板，按计划批量生成并发布。
6. 在个人中心查看授权、套餐、积分、订单，并通过微信扫码购买/续费；软件有新版本时自动检测并安装。

## English Overview

Wangaohui is a desktop tool for AI content creation and multi-platform publishing. It covers content scenarios including WeChat Official Accounts (公众号), Toutiao (头条号), Baijia (百家号) and Xiaohongshu image-post (贴图), and helps individual bloggers, agency operation teams and content creators run a single pipeline from topic & material selection, AI writing, template-based layout to multi-platform publishing.

Generated articles are natural with no AI trace, and pass AI detection 100%.

Account credentials and authoring data are stored locally and are not transmitted to third parties.

## English Features

- **Resource Management**
  - Today’s Hot Topics: real-time trending articles for topic selection and secondary creation
  - WeChat Viral Articles: aggregated viral posts from the WeChat ecosystem, one-click transfer into the creation workshop
  - Image Materials: platform-collected images with AI-generated tags, filterable and searchable by tag; tagged images can be used as article illustrations
  - My Images: personal image library with upload and custom tagging
  - My Materials: import TXT materials or generate content outlines via AI based on account positioning; materials are editable and usable for scheduled hosting
  - Materials are fully customizable: user-defined groups, batch import, AI generation, per-entry editing; materials are not limited to text and can be paired with specified layout / cover templates; hosting tasks can be bound directly

- **Creation Workshop · Article Mode**
  - Input a topic/material, AI generates: viral titles → article outline → full body → layout
  - Output is in a natural, human-written style with no AI trace, passing AI detection 100%
  - Built-in WeChat Official Account layout templates, plus a theme generator and custom templates (save as “My Templates”)
  - Fully customizable layout: modify color, font, font size and style parameters based on any built-in template, or fine-tune with custom CSS; save as “My Templates” and use them on equal footing with built-in ones
  - Auto illustrations: match from the image material library by article content, or pick from the personal library
  - Word count selectable at 800 / 1200 / 1500 or custom
  - Rich-text editing components: titles, quotes, cards, lists, dividers, CTA
  - Mobile-effect preview, copy HTML to the WeChat Official Account backend

- **Creation Workshop · Image-post Mode**
  - AI generates from the topic: image-post title + segmented body + hashtag
  - Cover template + content card template: the title is rendered on the cover, the body is rendered as cards segment by segment
  - 50+ built-in cover templates, categorized by style
  - Custom templates (advanced): upload background images, configure text areas (position / font size / color / alignment / line count), custom CSS, save as “My Templates” for use everywhere
  - Custom image-post materials: beyond built-in templates, users can upload their own cover/card backgrounds and freely define the title and body text regions, saving to a personal template library
  - Image-posts published to WeChat Official Account automatically include the hashtag in the body

- **Account Center**
  - Login support for WeChat Official Account, Toutiao and Baijia via QR code / web page (Xiaohongshu supports image-post publishing); cookies are saved automatically
  - WeChat Official Account supports syncing multiple accounts under the same name, with a currently active account marker
  - Per-account toggles, originality / first-release / AI statement and other configurations are persisted
  - One-click access to the platform backend

- **Content Publishing**
  - Unified article / image-post publishing: choose platform first, then account (linked); supports publishing to WeChat Official Account, Toutiao and Baijia; WeChat Official Account supports saving to the draft box; image-posts support publishing to Xiaohongshu
  - Paginated publishing history, filterable by platform / account
  - Generation records are linked with publishing status to avoid duplicate publishing

- **Scheduled Hosting**
  - Create timed tasks: daily / one-time, articles per successful run, start time + interval
  - Content sources: My Materials / Today’s Hot Topics / WeChat Viral Articles
  - Supports articles and image-posts (image-posts can be published to WeChat Official Account / Xiaohongshu); WeChat Official Account requires selecting a specific account
  - Used materials are automatically marked and will not be consumed again
  - Real-time task execution logs; failed runs retry automatically

- **Personal Center**
  - Authorization status, plan, expiration date and credits balance at a glance
  - Plan purchase / renewal: pay via WeChat QR code, takes effect automatically under the same authorization
  - Credit records: AI usage deduction history
  - Order records: purchase / renewal orders and payment status
  - Software update: auto-detect new versions, background download and auto-install

- **Security & Privacy**
  - Account cookies / login state are stored locally only
  - The AI gateway calls transmit only generation content and do not collect personal data
  - The installer does not embed any API keys
